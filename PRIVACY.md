# QA Inspector — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 11, 2026_

QA Inspector is a Chrome extension that helps designers, QA engineers and front-end developers compare a live web page with its Figma design. This policy explains what data the extension handles and how.

## What data the extension handles

**Figma personal access token.** If you choose to connect Figma, you enter your own Figma personal access token. The token is stored locally in your browser using Chrome's extension storage. It is sent only to the official Figma API (`api.figma.com`) to read the Figma files and frames you choose to compare. You can disconnect at any time, which removes the token.

**Web page content.** When you click "Inspect element" or "Scan page", the extension reads the styles and structure of the page in the active tab (for example CSS values, sizes, colors and design-token variables). This data is processed locally in your browser to show results in the side panel. It is not sent to the developer or to any third party.

**Settings.** Interface preferences such as language are stored locally in your browser.

## What the extension does not do

- It does not collect, store or transmit personal data to the developer.
- It does not use analytics, tracking or advertising.
- It does not sell or transfer user data to third parties.
- It does not use or transfer user data for purposes unrelated to its single purpose.
- It does not use or transfer user data to determine creditworthiness or for lending purposes.

## Third-party services

The only external service the extension communicates with is the Figma API, and only when you connect Figma and request a Figma file. Your use of Figma is governed by Figma's own privacy policy: https://www.figma.com/legal/privacy/

## Data retention and deletion

All data stays on your device. To delete it, disconnect Figma in the extension or remove the extension from Chrome, which clears its local storage.
